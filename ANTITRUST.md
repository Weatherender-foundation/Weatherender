# Antitrust Policy

Weatherender is developed as an independent open-source software project under
the **Apache License 2.0**. The project must not be used to coordinate unlawful
restrictions on competition or commercial conduct.

This policy applies to maintainers, contributors, sponsors, service providers, and participants in project spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, chat, and project meetings.

## Prohibited conduct

Participants must not use Weatherender or its community spaces to:

- agree on or coordinate prices, fees, discounts, salaries, commissions, or other commercial terms;
- divide customers, territories, suppliers, markets, products, or opportunities;
- coordinate bids, tenders, refusals to deal, boycotts, or supply restrictions;
- exchange non-public competitively sensitive information, including future pricing, capacity, costs, sales forecasts, customer lists, or business strategy;
- pressure another person or organization to adopt a commercial restriction or to exclude a competitor; or
- disguise a commercial agreement as a technical decision, roadmap item, license discussion, or interoperability proposal.

Technical collaboration is welcome when it is focused on the software, its documentation, interoperability, security, or community health. A technical discussion must not become a forum for coordinating independent commercial behavior.

## Handling concerns

If a discussion moves toward a prohibited topic:

1. Do not continue the discussion or share additional sensitive information.
2. State that the topic is out of scope and ask that it be stopped.
3. Notify the lead maintainer at [lehacomp16@gmail.com](mailto:lehacomp16@gmail.com).
4. Report harassment or other community violations under the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
5. Report security vulnerabilities under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), rather than in a public issue.

The lead maintainer may close, edit, or remove a discussion, issue, pull request, or comment that presents antitrust or other legal risk. The [Governance](GOVERNANCE.md) document describes project decision-making authority.

## Licensing and independence

The Weatherender license grants only the rights stated in [LICENSE](LICENSE). A contribution, integration, or mention in [adopters.md](adopters.md) does not create a partnership, exclusivity arrangement, reseller relationship, or agreement about prices or markets.

Nothing in this policy is legal advice. Participants are responsible for obtaining advice about the laws that apply to their own conduct. When in doubt, stop the discussion and seek qualified legal advice.
