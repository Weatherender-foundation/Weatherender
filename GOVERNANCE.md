# Project Governance

**Weatherender** is developed under the **Weatherender Foundation**.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day technical decisions are made by the lead maintainer.
- Major changes (architecture, licensing, breaking API changes, repository moves) require explicit approval from the lead maintainer.
- Community feedback is welcome and considered, but final authority rests with the maintainer(s).

## Roles

- **Lead Maintainer** — full control over the repository, releases, infrastructure, and license decisions.
- **Contributors** — anyone who submits code, documentation, or issues. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Maintainers** — listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Conflict resolution

In case of disagreement, the lead maintainer has the final say.
The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies to all interactions.

## Changes to this document

This governance model can be updated by the lead maintainer. Significant changes will be announced in a GitHub Discussion or in the release notes.
